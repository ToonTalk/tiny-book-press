Fold it into a book

  1. Print landscape, single-sided, at Actual size / 100%.
  2. Crease along every dashed line, both ways.
  3. Cut only the solid line in the middle.
  4. Fold the sheet in half the long way, with the printing outside.
  5. Push the two ends toward each other. The slit opens into a cross.
  6. Flatten it into a book with page 1 in front and page 8 at the back.
